Operational Transition

OMR sheets are out. Screen speed decides ranks.

The Union Education Ministry finalized the computer-based format to eliminate paper leaks, tampering, and physical bubbling errors. Interface literacy is now a competitive advantage.

OMR Paper Paradigm (2004–2026)

Paper Friction & Inversion Risks

  • ✕ Bubbling Tax: Marking 180 bubbles takes 18–22 minutes of pure mechanical wrist time.
  • ✕ Irreversible Mistakes: Fill Option B instead of C by mistake and you lose 5 marks with zero remedy.
  • ✕ Bubble Displacement: An accidental offset at Question 40 offsets all subsequent answers on the row.
  • ✕ Vulnerability: Physical paper transit created systemic security risks across exam centers.
NTA CBT Simulator Paradigm (2027+)

Instant Telemetry & Error Correction

  • ✓ 18 Minutes Saved: Instant clicks give you 20+ minutes back for complex calculation review.
  • ✓ Free Answer Revision: Modify or clear any chosen option right up until the final second.
  • ✓ Mark for Review: Filter doubtful questions directly in the color-coded question palette.
  • ✓ Randomized Item Delivery: Shuffled question orders eliminate neighbor-view bias.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the 2027 CBT shift and the practice simulator.

Is the NEET CBT simulator completely free?
Yes. The simulator is completely free and open source under the MIT license. There are no paywalls, no required sign-ups, and no advertising trackers.
Is this the official NTA website?
No. This is an independent open-source practice project engineered to replicate the NTA computer-based examination interface. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Testing Agency (NTA) or the National Medical Commission (NMC).
Does the application work offline?
Yes. The application is built as an offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA). Once loaded in your browser, all assets and question banks are cached locally in your browser storage.
How do I import custom question banks or PYQs?
You can import standard JSON question files containing question text, 4 options, correct answer indices, and subject identifiers directly from the home screen import button.
Why is NEET UG transitioning to computer-based testing in 2027?
Following committee reviews on examination reforms, computer-based testing was chosen to ensure tamper-proof randomized question generation, eliminate logistics vulnerabilities associated with physical paper transportation, and remove OMR bubbling errors.
